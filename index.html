01 — About

A bit about me

I'm a frontend developer who cares about the boring parts: markup that makes sense, layouts that survive a 320px screen, and pages that load before you notice.

I started out rebuilding sites I liked to figure out how they worked, and that habit stuck. Today I spend most of my time writing HTML and CSS from scratch, then layering on just enough JavaScript to make things feel alive — theme toggles, filters, form validation, that sort of thing.

Right now I'm looking for a frontend internship or junior role where I can ship real features with people who review code properly.
